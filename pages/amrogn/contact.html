Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer delivery?

Yes — each branch handles its own delivery within a few kilometers. Call your nearest branch to check if you're in range.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash and mobile money transfers at all branches. Card payment is available at select locations.

Can I place a large or catering order?

Absolutely. For catering or bulk orders, call ahead or use the contact form above so we can prepare in time.

What are your opening hours?

All Amrogn Chicken branches are open daily from 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM.
